Meet Your Teacher

Gavin Hammond

Composer. Performer. Teacher. Audio Engineer.

With over 30 years at the piano and a music degree from the Los Angeles Film School, Gavin Hammond brings a rare breadth of expertise to every lesson. He is a working composer, stage performer, audio engineer, and sound designer — meaning he doesn't just teach music, he lives it.

An instructor at the Radio Record and Film Connection, Gavin has guided students from complete beginners to advanced performers across virtually every genre. Whether you're 6 or 60, picking up the keys for the first time or refining technique you've built for years, Gavin meets you exactly where you are.

"My lessons are not just about playing the notes but understanding the music's soul. I tailor my approach to each student's level, learning style, and musical interests."

— Gavin Hammond

What You'll Learn

Every Style. Every Level.

From Bach to Beyoncé — Gavin teaches it all with equal passion and depth.

  • Classical

    Bach, Beethoven, Chopin — master the foundations with proper technique, posture, and sight-reading.

  • Jazz

    Improvisation, chord voicings, standards, and the art of musical conversation — unlock the jazz mindset.

  • Pop & Rock

    Play your favorite songs. Learn contemporary chord patterns, lead lines, and the feel of modern music.

  • Gospel, R&B & Hip-Hop

    Soulful chord progressions, rhythmic feel, and the groove that makes music move people.

  • Music Theory

    Understand the language behind the music — scales, harmony, rhythm, and how it all connects.

  • Composition & Songwriting

    Learn to create your own music — from initial ideas to finished songs, with guidance from a working composer.
